Who I Am

One person, three skill stacks

I'm Noor Ul Hassan — better known online as Gx_Rajput — a self-driven creative professional from Layyah, Punjab, Pakistan. For more than seven years I have designed brand identities, edited videos, and shipped software for clients locally and online.

Most designers stop at the artwork. I take it further: I design it, animate it, and then build the website or app that carries it. That means you get a brand that looks right and works right — without coordinating three different freelancers.

  • Design: logos, social media posts, thumbnails, banners, business cards & complete brand kits.
  • Video: cinematic edits, reels, intros, colour grading, weddings, ads and YouTube content.
  • Development: full-stack websites (React, Node), mobile apps (Flutter, React Native) and Python automation.
  • Print: on demand — flyers, billboards, packaging, letterheads & stationery (printing charges separate).
